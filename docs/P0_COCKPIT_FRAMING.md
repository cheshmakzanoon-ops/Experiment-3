# P0 — retained RB19/R06 cockpit framing

## Observed defect and composition study

The unmodified production renderer at `0cfbdae22fb58c6bff63fcb4b86509fb48e67926` was loaded with the actual supplied binary, full circuit and production simulation. Its captured halo crossbar intersects the distant-road band, and the lower steering controls are cropped. This is a camera composition defect, not missing cockpit art.

- [Baseline capture](https://github.com/cheshmakzanoon-ops/Experiment-3/actions/runs/36675650525)
- [Five-position in-game eye study](https://github.com/cheshmakzanoon-ops/Experiment-3/actions/runs/36676018420)

The study retained the exact same simulation snapshot and original geometry. It compared the source eye, lower/back, lower/deep, back-only and higher/back positions. Those temporary study offsets were test-only and never changed the runtime. Lower/deep excessively reduced near-road visibility; higher/back looked over the halo and resembled a pod camera; back-only retained the obstructed horizon. Lower/back was selected for further validation because it separates the halo crossbar from the road and retains the complete wheel and both mirrors.

## Runtime calibration

`src/rendering/cockpit-framing.ts` applies a chassis-space offset of 50 mm down and 120 mm back from the retained supplied eye socket. The original socket, binary, mesh transforms, skeleton, head visibility, steering animation and 250 mm suspension-datum conversion remain unchanged. The camera follows chassis orientation and receives the datum once.

The seated cockpit has its own 64-degree vertical field of view and -0.045-radian pitch. It no longer inherits pod speed widening. The pod and chase lens/positions retain their existing behavior. Look controls still override the driver's gaze through the normal input path.

The inertial spring remains simulation-clock driven. Only the supplied cockpit's applied displacement is bounded to ±20 mm lateral, ±12 mm vertical and ±18 mm longitudinal about its calibrated origin. Even an unusually large impact cannot push the applied eye outside that volume. Clamping does not mutate the recorded frame, source socket or spring state. Pause and rewind remain deterministic.

## Evidence and acceptance

The permanent `p0-cockpit-framing.spec.ts` survey uses the normal asset loader and complete production renderer with medium graphics, shadows and mirrors. It retains screenshots and measurements for 16:9, 4:3, 16:10 and 21:9 framing; both actual steering locks; pause and rewind; moving and braking frames; a driven kerb contact; sunset, night and wet night; and the unchanged pod view. Wetness and kerb contact come from the production simulation rather than edited frame channels. Nine near-plane rays test actual visible car triangles; steering display projection, source-frame integrity and fixed cockpit FOV are also checked.

The initial source-camera baseline passed its limited capture checks. The complete pre-calibration local check passed 1,400 tests in 144 files, lint, TypeScript and production build. The new camera unit tests pass 39 focused cases together with the existing camera tests. Candidate image inspection, full final check and exact-commit CI are separate gates and are recorded below when complete.

Local Chromium process sockets and localhost cloud-browser access are restricted in this executor, so GPU evidence is produced in the repository's normal GitHub Actions environment. These software-rendered images and deterministic sample drives are not a continuous human-driven lap or representative-hardware performance certification. `finalArtApproved` remains false, and this repair does not certify the environment as photorealistic.
