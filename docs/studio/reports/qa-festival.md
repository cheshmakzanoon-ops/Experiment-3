1. WHAT WAS BUILT
scripts/studio/tools/festival-qa.ts (importable, CLI at the bottom). Headless, no renderer. Per activity it runs: arrive (HUD-style fast travel, or the pilot drives there) -> marker card or entry gate -> Start -> run -> finish/fail -> typed result -> career settlement (settleFestival, src/career/festival.ts) -> festival save (recordFestivalBest + validateFestivalSave, JSON round trip) -> a RELOADED session built from that save -> next activity. Production code driven unmodified: OpenWorldSimulation (the open-world worker's session), setInput (worker input path), settleFestival, festival-save. The player stand-in is Pilot = the festival RouteDriver writing the pedals of whichever car is the player's, pressing reset when stuck. Per run it records completed, time, position, stars, score, reward and reputation, stuck time, stall, NaN/Infinity (pose, speed, every number in the result), pilot resets vs session recoveries vs rival resets, contacts, falls, traffic collisions, off-route seconds, incident lines with route metre and position, and ~16 result-consistency checks (event/machine named, run id unique, stars = star rule, legal distance covers the route, race position = rank in standings = 1 + earlier finishers, standings sorted, player time = standings time, trap/zone speeds below top speed and consistent with time and distance, payout lines sum, same run never paid twice, career record, best saved, save survives JSON, reload knows discoveries, field back to one car after Continue). Every record carries seed and repro command. Skill challenges are entered from a 260 m road run-up at speed (production now has the same planner, see D6). Modes: --events/--vehicles/--weathers/--seed matrix, --approach drive (+ --traffic), --tour, --leaks N, --slide, --trace t0,t1, --summarize. 18 events (docs said 16; OPEN_WORLD.md corrected): road-race 1 (coast-to-summit), circuit 5 (grand-loop, port-ilse-loop, kestrel-circuit, saltmarsh-club-race, saltmarsh-practice), sprint 3 (valley-sprint, thornwood-rally, harbour-welcome), showcase 1 (runway-showcase), speed-trap 3 (ridge-trap, coast-trap, runway-launch), speed-zone 2 (pass-zone, plateau-zone), drift-zone 3 (hairpin-drift, quarry-drift, kestrel-drift). The passive driving skill chain (FestivalSkillChain) builds by itself in free roam; the tour recorded session totals up to 179,792 points (best chain 47,195).

3. DEFECTS FOUND (ranked), all fixed at the root with a failing-then-passing test unless marked
D1 HIGH, game: a hypercar flat out on the Grand Loop ("flat out" ring) pitched up and ROLLED OVER with no driver input at Ridge Highway (-885,878), route m 7732-7811, t=114.8 s. Root cause: the grade-separated crossing pins the over-road deck (road-network.ts profile()); the grade-clamped approach descends at max grade to a pinned deck lower than both neighbours, a V of 11 % grade change in 12 m; at 58 m/s that is 3 g compression, vy 3.4 -> +6.9 m/s, pitch 1.5 rad/s, the car leaves the road and lands upside down. Fix: raise-only curvature-limited fillet at an over-road's deck (SAG_ACCELERATION 4 m/s2 at 1.25 x design speed). Deck there rises 0.45 m (7.42 m -> 7.87 m above the valley road, still under the 8 m layer overlap). Test fixture constants in tests/festival-ground-height.test.ts updated for that deck height (95.139 -> 95.588) and the overlap hint made (below+deck)/2 instead of below+3.5. Tests: highway max sag < 0.0075/m (was 0.0124) and an EH-3 at 55 m/s stays upright (up.y min was -1.0).
D2 HIGH, AI+game: route driver looked 21 samples = 252 m ahead (circuit AI constant) but 88 m/s needs 270 m at 1.4 g, so it braked at the limit of its horizon and ran wide at the T-junction ending the Coast Highway (-3484,-1985, m 12204): Grand Loop had 2 pilot resets, 3 rival resets, 5.2 s off route per lap. Fix: twice the look-ahead above 55 m/s (route-driver.ts). Grand Loop now 0 resets / 0 s off route, 253 s. Test: from 86 m/s the car stays on the 7.6 m half-width road.
D3 HIGH (weather pillar), AI: straight-line pace ignored grip: in rain/storm an EH-3 held ~290 km/h on the plateau road (grip estimate 0.19 of dry), could not steer back from the crown and left the road: plateau-zone FAIL rain and storm, thornwood-rally storm FAIL. Fix: pace factor clamp(0.3 + 1.6 x grip, 0.4, 1) x top speed (grip >= 0.44 untouched). Tests: plateau-zone completes with 0 resets in rain, storm, snow. Short-event weather matrix went from 3 FAIL to 0.
D4 MEDIUM, AI: after backing out of a jam the gearbox stays in reverse until speed < 1.5 m/s (vehicle.ts), and the driver pressed throttle: the car reversed away from the route at 10 m/s (Harbour Opening Run in rain: 25 s lost, 2 resets). Fix: brake to a stop before driving on. Test with a car rolling backwards.
D5 MEDIUM, AI (rivals and autopilot too): `stuck > 9` could never be reached because every recovery clears `stuck`, so a car wedged 10 m off the road (inside RouteProgress tolerance) sat for 300+ s asking for nothing (Port Ilse Street Loop, storm: a 3-lap race timing out at 540 s). Fix: progress watchdog (no 3 m of route progress in 12 s while it wants to move -> asks for reset) and the autopilot now resets during events. Test: held car resets within 40 s (never before).
