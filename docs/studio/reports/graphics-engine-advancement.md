## Audit priorities and engineering decisions

The highest-risk graphics finding was **rendered-state consistency across ownership and replay boundaries**: a newly selected tier could outlive its scene, shared pixels could be released by one consumer, a rider could receive physical suspension movement twice, and prepared textures could be uploaded after their renderer was retired. These faults produce disappearing scenery, contact errors, visible pops and startup failures. The implementation therefore connects resource ownership, physical pose, recorded state and real GPU assertions before claiming a higher visual tier.

The highest-risk physics finding was **inconsistent contact information between the physical plant and its controls**. Road and GP bikes now change vertical support continuously, but several controls still assumed constant gravity, stale force history or instantaneously sustainable reload peaks. Improving suspension without correcting those assumptions exposed falls in existing races. The contact, braking, recovery and admission changes are evaluated against native force limits and the original events, with failed candidates retained in the evidence. More grip or more permissive fall thresholds would conceal the inconsistency.

The largest remaining visual gap is **authored art and scene composition at the intended viewing distance**. The available Low software-rendered images show the full application working, but cannot certify High lighting, surface response, vehicle detail or a PS5-like finish. The next visual review must use completed High/Ultra views and a named physical GPU, and prioritize materials, foliage silhouettes, trackside composition and vehicle/rider presentation together. The present work establishes and tests those systems; final art approval remains a separate judgment.

## What changed

### Scene quality, lighting and motion

**Vegetation and quality transitions now have explicit ownership.** A quality change prepares and compiles a complete planting tier, then installs it atomically. Cancellation and rollback retain a valid scene. Tier changes preserve placement determinism, shrubs and the stable scene root; near-detail selection responds to a stationary camera when switching between High and Ultra. Low and Medium no longer construct the invisible High motocross meadow. Full offroad owners dispose detached resources, queued terrain and roost work, hidden fallback assets and late callbacks. Shared decoded texture pixels remain usable by the resident cache when one scene releases its GPU texture handle; exclusively owned bitmaps are closed once. Sources: [`vegetation`](../../../src/rendering/vegetation/), [offroad ownership](../../OFFROAD_RESOURCE_OWNERSHIP.md), [texture ownership](../../TEXTURE_SOURCE_OWNERSHIP.md).

**Festival traffic has differentiated original silhouettes and correct wheel motion.** Ten fixed vehicle profiles share nine native instance morph targets. Four body batches and one wheel batch retain a five-batch bound. Independent front-wheel steering uses Ackermann geometry; wheel rotation follows authoritative signed longitudinal travel rather than sideways displacement. Held and replayed frames are reproducible. The traffic auxiliary payload records this travel explicitly. Sources: [`traffic-car-geometry.ts`](../../../src/rendering/traffic-car-geometry.ts), [`traffic-cars.ts`](../../../src/rendering/traffic-cars.ts), [`ambient-traffic.ts`](../../../src/simulation/open-world/ambient-traffic.ts).

**Terrain changes continuously while keeping coverage.** Festival terrain uses native position and normal morphs, with matching material masks and shading. Refinement installs complete child quartets; coarsening reverses the transition before retiring children. Parent and children do not leave holes or draw overlapping coverage. Road-adjacent geometry and nearby tree placement align with the settled contact surface. Disposal, stale worker replies and temporary cover retention during budget reductions are handled explicitly. Sources: [`terrain.ts`](../../../src/rendering/open-world/terrain.ts), [`terrain-morph.ts`](../../../src/rendering/open-world/terrain-morph.ts), [`terrain-material.ts`](../../../src/rendering/open-world/terrain-material.ts).

**Night lighting and reflections have bounded update policies.** Selected street lamps cast local shadows on supported nearby occluders. High uses one map, Ultra two, and lower tiers none. Assignment changes fade predictably, held scenes reuse their maps, and shadow work is scoped around the main scene pass. Local reflection probes refresh for world movement and scenery changes, capture ordinary updates one face per frame, and publish a complete cube from one fixed capture origin. Sources: [`street-light-pools.ts`](../../../src/rendering/open-world/street-light-pools.ts), [reflection contract](../../LOCAL_REFLECTION_REFRESH.md).

**Temporal accumulation rejects incompatible geometry history.** The existing history alpha channel now holds view depth. The resolve uses the last successfully rendered projection and rigid transforms, rejects incompatible history texels before filtering, and keeps valid history accumulation. Reset, camera cut, held frame, seek and failure boundaries are explicit; final composite output remains opaque. This adds validation reads without adding another history target or resolve pass. Sources: [`temporal-aa.ts`](../../../src/rendering/studio/temporal-aa.ts), [depth-rejection contract](../../TEMPORAL_DEPTH_REJECTION.md).

**Startup prepares the state that will actually be rendered.** The renderer sets the venue's real light slots before compilation, removing a demonstrated redundant daylight shader variant. Cold venue-field construction is now cooperative and precedes the real scene constructor. Completed-only caching, cancellation and synchronous takeover preserve exact field and meadow output. This improves scheduling; the measurements do not establish a guaranteed frame-time ceiling. Sources: [startup lighting](../../STARTUP_LIGHTING_PREPARATION.md), [venue-field preparation](../../VENUE_FIELD_PREPARATION.md).

### Motorcycle contact, controls and visible pose

**Road motorcycles now have sprung heave and pitch with unilateral support.** The two-contact solver integrates springs, damping, preload, progressive bump response, actual compression and free-wheel gaps. An unloaded tyre cannot create ground drive or braking. A bike can leave the road, follow a ballistic trajectory and land; external vertical and pitch impulses enter the physical state. The published chassis transform contains the resulting height, pitch and vertical motion. This is a reduced sprung model, without separate unsprung wheel masses. Sources: [`road-bike-suspension.ts`](../../../src/simulation/road-bike-suspension.ts), [solver contract](../../road-bike-suspension.md).

**Contact selection no longer accepts a distant runoff surface over the local road.** A reproduced Ardennes case selected a broad runoff triangle over 34 m away and introduced a false 13.83 cm step. The correction applies local surface ownership before nearest-hit pruning, including finite tyre-footprint queries. It retains layer separation for stacked roads and bridges. Road suspension samples the continuous collision ribbon, with the established analytic fallback beyond it. Sources: [`contact.ts`](../../../src/simulation/contact.ts), [`bvh.ts`](../../../src/simulation/bvh.ts), [ownership evidence](../../ROAD_CONTACT_OWNERSHIP.md).

**Road control no longer treats a brief reload spike as sustained cornering support.** The controller admits rising support through its existing 9 s⁻¹ response and accepts falling support immediately. This filter affects new lean demand and stopping-distance planning, while actual contact loads, tyre forces and instantaneous recovery authority remain unfiltered. Six prescribed transient-support counterexamples fail before the change and survive afterward. The original Arroyo eight-bike, five-lap race changes from 29 falls to zero without changing its course, seed, field, deadline, fall bound, tyre curve or rider skill. All affected strict steady-state calibration values remain bit-identical. [Arroyo evidence](arroyo-road-support-admission.md).

**Vellamar's authored elevation transitions preserve road grade.** The earlier periodic profile interpolator had a derivative discontinuity that produced a suspension/control disturbance even with continuous height. The correction aligns transition values and slopes, retains the authored endpoints and closes the original reproduced race failure. [Profile and contact evidence](../../VELLAMAR_PROFILE_CONTINUITY.md).

**GP contact integration preserves planar momentum and current force capacity.** Forward and sideways momentum transform together as the bike yaws. The front steering inverse matches the forward combined-slip projection and signed longitudinal-force contribution. A rear-force preview uses the current contact preparation without changing physical history. Roll, drive and braking reserves account for the requested recovery demand rather than hiding it behind already saturated output. Motorcycle braking plans use actual offset-path distance, grade and signed net deceleration. Sources: [`bike.ts`](../../../src/simulation/bike.ts), [`motogp-physics.ts`](../../../src/simulation/motogp-physics.ts), [`bike-ai.ts`](../../../src/simulation/bike-ai.ts), [`motorcycle-braking-plan.ts`](../../../src/simulation/motorcycle-braking-plan.ts).

**The final GP correction preserves equivalent elastic tyre strain across load changes.** Retaining lateral force in absolute newtons shed force immediately on unloading but rebuilt it slowly on reloading. The corrected history carries `Fy / corneringStiffness` into the current contact before the unchanged relaxation and friction limit. Constant preparation retains the original arithmetic; zero contact clears force and history; repeated previews remain read-only. A prescribed alternating-load bench increased mean lateral force from −853.18 N to −1,148.69 N, close to the independent steady-load comparison of −1,141.61 N. The nonlinear model does not require equality with constant mean support. No tyre curve, friction coefficient, relaxation length, controller gain, AI skill or crash limit was increased. [Contact relaxation evidence](../../GP_CONTACT_RELAXATION.md).

**Braking and ordinary-road lean control now use compatible geometry and budgets.** The anti-stoppie bound comes from the current chassis pitch moment arms and applies to the combined service and engine-braking force. Nominal road lean intent remains separate from actual recovery capacity, including individual wheel grip. The roll controller integrates the remaining opposing moment and accounts for the existing rate tracker's response distance. These corrections resolve the GP/SBK stopping-distance and NR-900 cornering-band failures while retaining the original handling targets. [Calibration report](engine-advancement-calibration.md).

**The rendered bike uses the physical chassis movement once.** The earlier rig added visual fork/shock heave and pitch to a root that already contained the new physical movement. A production regression measured a 21.65 mm duplicated displacement. Marked road snapshots now place the sprung model, seated rider and cockpit in the physical CG frame. The wheels, fork, swingarm, chain and grounding projector consume measured road planes and wheel gaps; flight no longer raises an inferred road plane or attaches a false contact shadow. The tests inspect actual rubber-mesh vertices, rider contact points and camera sockets. A subsequent GPU review also found a garment quaternion half-turn ambiguity: a 0.12 mm posture change could move a pants ring by 110 mm. The local correction resolves only that numerical tie; nearby-pose skin movement is about 0.045 mm afterward. The corrected first-presentation/seek contract subsequently passed the actual GPU job at `f1068433`: held, restored and rewind images all had zero changed pixels and zero maximum channel difference. [Presentation contract](../../road-bike-presentation-contract.md).

### Thermal behavior, catalogue response, collisions and replay

**Motorcycle temperatures follow work and environmental exchange.** Two physical tyre/rotor networks own the state; the four-slot vehicle interface mirrors front/front and rear/rear values without counting power twice. Slip heat comes from represented relative contact motion. Rolling heat follows the actual rolling-resistance work. Rotor heating excludes engine braking and stops when rotor rotation stops. Airborne tyres receive no ground-friction heat. The solver exchanges heat with air, road and water instead of moving tyres toward an ideal temperature; core temperature drives hot pressure. The reduced road/supermoto path does not invent lateral slip heat that it cannot resolve. [Thermal model and limits](../../MOTORCYCLE_THERMAL.md).

**Catalogue cars have differentiated authored hardware response.** Thirteen hardware families supply camber gain, roll-centre and small anti-geometry terms, intake response and shift interruption for 46 catalogue cars. APX-01 and TC-1 retain dedicated blocks; explicit overrides, turbo engine blocks, kart geometry and motorcycle defaults remain authoritative. Tyre curves, torque curves, masses and original design bands are unchanged. These are authored fictional-machine parameters, not manufacturer measurements. [Catalogue hardware](../../CATALOGUE_HARDWARE.md).

**Festival static collision checks cover the complete footprint and finite height.** Bounded cell queries include all intersected cells and deduplicate results. Exact rectangle overlap handles containment and crossed edges that point sampling missed. Trunk-centre contacts have finite deterministic normals. Oriented vertical bounds prevent a vehicle on another level from colliding with a tree or building footprint below or above it. This retains the existing impulse and penetration limits; it is not a new swept 3D collision manifold. [Static contact contract](../../FESTIVAL_STATIC_CONTACTS.md).

**Flashback preserves controls when AI hands control back.** Input deduplication now compares both the last message and the live mutable controls. An unchanged player message is retained when the AI has overwritten the applied state; repeated shift/DRS/overtake edges are also retained. A reproduced live/replay divergence changed from 110 different frame fields to zero. Tests reconstruct GP, SBK, MX and car states, compare unrounded hidden state, continue the simulations and branch their journals. [Replay contract](../../ENGINE_REPLAY_CONTRACTS.md).

## Remaining boundaries and next visual priorities

The implementation establishes stronger foundations for a high-quality racing presentation. The following work remains material to a PS5-like result:

1. **Art quality and scene composition.** Consistent vehicle/rider materials, environment meshes, foliage silhouettes, terrain detail, trackside density and authored lighting still need review in actual High/Ultra gameplay. More post-processing alone cannot establish the intended art standard.
2. **Physical-device performance.** Measure the changed build on named target GPUs at explicit resolutions, with a populated race, wet conditions, cockpit views and long sessions. Software-GPU screenshots and unit timings cannot support a 60 FPS claim.
3. **Remaining renderer limits.** Local street-lamp shadows still omit terrain, asphalt and bridge decks as new casters. Temporal rejection does not provide complete per-vertex motion for every skinned rider, wheel or animated leaf. Reflection triggers have intentional latency and capture costs.
4. **Remaining simulation limits.** Road bikes use reduced heave/pitch and contact relaxation rather than full spatial suspension and unsprung masses. Ordinary road/supermoto has no complete lateral contact-slip state. Player and AI motocross still use different ground models. Festival static contacts are not swept full-3D manifolds.

The new GPU workflow has read-only repository permissions and cannot publish the game. The existing full CI and validated-release workflows remain authoritative for website/release deployment. Main-branch code publication, a passing local test command, and a deployed release are separate outcomes; this report claims only the ones supported by the recorded evidence.
