# Vehicle Art & Animation audit

Branch `studio/vehicle-art-audit` (cut from main at a074ea5). Auditor: Vehicle Art & Animation agent.
Everything below was measured on this branch; "not tested" is stated where it applies.

## 1. Scope

57 machines are selectable in the menu (the `#vehicle` select has 60 options; the two mixed-class
entries and the `bikes` mix are not machines): 48 four-wheel (APX-01, 37 closed bodies, 7 open-wheel,
2 karts, 1 buggy) and 9 motorcycles (6 road/GP, 3 off-road). Each was rendered in the real menu showroom
and looked at by eye (not only by metric). Animation was probed numerically with real simulation
frames (section 4). Hero-vehicle proposals are in section 6.

## 2. Tools delivered

| File                                     | What it does                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |

| `scripts/studio/tools/vehicle-drive.mjs` | In-session driving capture (menu -> start -> keys -> chase/cockpit shots). **Partly verified only**, see section 5.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| `tests/vehicle-pose-probe.test.ts`       | Numeric animation probe (section 4); also a regression test. `POSE_PROBE_OUT=file.json` dumps the table.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |

Throughput under a loaded 4-CPU host (load 6-16): boot to menu 85 s, then 9-28 s per machine; all 57
machines in 11-13 minutes. The showroom orbit is deterministic: APX-01 was pixel-identical between two
runs (mean abs diff 0.000), so before/after pairs are a fair A/B.

Gotcha found while building it: in a fresh worktree `public/models/supplied-player-lods.bin.gz` does not
exist (gitignored, generated by `npm run generate:player-lods`, 4 s). `vite preview` answers the missing
file with the HTML fallback and the game stops on "Player LOD integrity check failed"; the first capture
attempt hung 13 minutes on the loading screen before this was diagnosed. Run the generator once per worktree.

## 3. Captures and findings

Final captures: `docs/studio/reports/vehicle-screens/<id>.jpg` (57 of 57, 26-46 KB each, 2.0 MB).
Baselines of the machines that changed visibly: `before-<id>.jpg` (36 kept; unchanged baselines were
deleted to keep the repo small). `manifest.json` / `before-manifest.json` list them. Nothing failed or was
skipped. All captures are SwiftShader (software GL): reflections and bloom will differ in magnitude on a
GPU, so judgements about specular hot spots are qualitative.

Not found: z-fighting at the body/lamp/lamp-cover interfaces, floating wheels, wrong-scale bodies or blank
liveries. Measured on the 47 catalogue bodies (APX-01 is the supplied asset and was skipped) by building
the near LOD headless: tyre radius equals the definition on all 47, hub position error is 0.000 m on all,
and body length is within -15 % / +6 % of the collision box (a physics envelope with margins, not the
visual spec; the outliers are the buggy -15 %, XF-2 -12 %, SK-125 -11 %, SC-7 -10 %). A downward raycast
put the arch roof within 0-2 cm of the tyre top on DX-8 (rear), XF-2 (rear), RP-6 (rear), GB-4 and RXE
(front) - low confidence (it can hit a fender lip), not confirmed visually. These are geometry numbers
plus eyeballing; a sub-millimetre z-fight on a GPU would not show in a software-GL still.

Best: GP-R1/GP-R2 (rider, tyres, brake disc, fork and decals all coherent), MX-450/MX-250/SM-450 (fork
gold, number plates, rider boots), APX-01 (hero asset, 1.45 M triangles, readable livery), SC-7 and RH-3

## 4. Animation probe (numbers, not images)

`tests/vehicle-pose-probe.test.ts` runs a real `Simulation` (production AI as the player, 24-26 s,
snapshots every 1/30 s: 720-780 frames per machine), feeds each snapshot to the production render classes
(`FormulaCar`, `VehicleBodies`, `BikeShells`) and reads the posed `Object3D` transforms. Result on this
branch (`POSE_PROBE_OUT`):

| Quantity                                                 | APX-01 (FormulaCar)  | EH-3 (catalogue car)           | GP-R1                           | MX-450             |
| -------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------- | ------------------------------ | ------------------------------- | ------------------ |
| Peak speed in the run                                    | 81.6 m/s             | 71.5 m/s                       | 73.9 m/s                        | 23.6 m/s           |
| Steering wheel / handlebar vs recorded steer (Pearson r) | -1.000               | -1.000                         | 1.000                           | 1.000              |
| Steering ratio (wheel rad per steer rad)                 | 2.2                  | 14.3 (2.28 turns lock to lock) | n/a                             | n/a                |
| Front wheel yaw vs recorded `W.STEER`, max error         | 0                    | 0                              | n/a                             | n/a                |
| Wheel spin: rendered advance / recorded advance          | 1.000                | 1.000                          | 1.000 / 1.000                   | 1.000 / 1.000      |
| Suspension: hub (or fork) travel range                   | 39.5 mm              | 22.6 mm                        | 59.3 mm                         | 219 mm             |
| Suspension r vs recorded travel                          | -0.999               | -0.976                         | 1.000                           | 1.000              |
| Chassis roll range                                       | 2.1 deg (peak 3.4 g) | 2.5 deg (peak 1.06 g)          | 105 deg, r = -1.000 to lean cmd | 69 deg, r = -1.000 |
| Rider torso roll range                                   | n/a                  | n/a                            | 112 deg                         | 56 deg             |

| Glove to rim, full-lock sweep (after D3)                 | n/a                  | 0.0002 m max                   | n/a                             | n/a                |
| Forearm/upper-arm length error (after D3)                | n/a                  | 0                              | n/a                             | n/a                |

Reading it: steering, steered wheels, wheel rotation, suspension and bike lean all update from the
snapshot with the right sign and unit gain; the rider's hands stay at a constant offset from the grips
through a 105 degree lean range. Two observations rather than defects: (a) the MX-450's `sprung` group
never pitches (range 0.000 rad) on purpose, `DirtBikeModel.pose` keeps the body rigid to the simulated
root and articulates only hubs, so chassis pitch comes from the root quaternion; (b) catalogue-car body
roll is small (2-2.5 deg) and only weakly correlated with lateral G (|r| 0.10-0.15), so the chase camera
shows an almost level car even at 3.4 g (APX-01). That is the simulation's pose, not a render bug, but a
presentation roll gain would add motion feel.

Brake lights work on cars. There are no turn indicators or hazards on any catalogue body or bike
(only ambient traffic has them); headlamps are only the emissive lens level (`headlights` flag).

Not probed: the **default APX-01 as the game really draws it** is the supplied player GLB
(`SuppliedPlayer.update`: steering clip, hubs, spins). Its code shares the same sampling helpers
(`wheelPhase`, `contactHubLength`, `steeringSample`) and was read, but the asset needs a GPU/ImageBitmap
decode and was not instantiated headless. Gear/clutch hand motion on bikes (`gpBrakeFingers`, shifter) was
not sampled.

## 6. Proposed hero-vehicle upgrades (ranked for Steam screenshot appeal)

1. **EH-3 Electric Hypercar (festival default).** It is the first machine of the headline
   discipline yet it is the generic `supercar` sheet. The pressed-flank kit that makes SC-7 and RH-3 the best
   closed bodies is data-driven and absent from `eh3` in `body-sheet.ts`: add `frontSweep`, `rearSweep` and
   `sideSculpt` (SC-7 uses 0.28/0.24/0.042) and the real lamp pockets, bonnet outlets and pocketed side duct
   (`festival-nose.ts`, `festival-rear.ts`) switch on instead of the flat raised scoop. Then give it an
   electric identity: a continuous emissive front/rear light bar in the menu's cyan in place of the three-dot
   `slim` lamps, a two-tone pearl-over-carbon paint (`paint: 'metallic'` flakes already exist), the
   articulated rear aero from `solvane-details.ts` raised in the showroom, and blue-lit wheel arches.
   Expected effect: a distinct silhouette in the one car every festival screenshot features. Cost: a day;
   risk: `festival-vehicle-detail`/`festival-side-construction` tests cover SC-7 geometry and must be
   extended for EH-3.
2. **APX-01 (circuit default and the car on the capsule).** The asset is strong but (a) its provenance is
   D4, which has to be settled before it ships; (b) the front-wing flaps and floor go high-key at the low
   showroom eye (D8): lower the composite clear coat or add a roughness floor on near-horizontal skins in
   `supplied-lookdev.ts` and update the pinned `[0.6, 0.08]` test with that stated reason; (c) a team livery
   with a readable original sponsor set on the sidepod and wing (the `VOLTEX` mark already works) and a
   rim-light in the showroom so the navy body separates from the black backdrop (D9).
3. **GP-R1 corner shot.** The best rider/bike pair in the game and already animated correctly (105 degree
   lean range, hands constant to the grips). A hero frame needs three additions: a lean-in-corner photo-mode
   preset at 55-60 degrees, a visible knee slider with a spark burst, and the carbon-disc glow
   (`gpDiscGlow`) at braking. Replace the SS-600/SBK-1000 egg cowl (D5) with the GP shell's proportions so the
   whole Motorcycles group reaches that bar.

## 9. Known limits

- **In-session capture (`vehicle-drive.mjs`) did not produce images on this host.** Festival: the session
  starts and shows its HUD (about 100 s) but SwiftShader cannot complete a page screenshot within 240 s once
  the open world is drawing. GP (GP-R1): attempt 1 ended on the game's own "SOMETHING WENT WRONG - SESSION
  STOPPED" screen after about 400 s (no console error was captured; the 45 s accumulated physics-worker
  watchdog in `main.ts` is the likely trigger on a starved CPU, unconfirmed); attempt 2 was still at
  "WARMING UP TYRES 94 %" after 300 s. I did not run the same scenario against the baseline build, so I
  cannot say whether this is purely host load; no code on the GP path was changed (bikes, `BikeShells`
  and the worker are untouched) and the catalogue-body change ran for 24 s of real simulation frames in the
  probe without error. The tool is committed for a GPU host and its output is unverified. The numeric probe
  (section 4) replaces it for animation evidence; the cockpit-view hands are therefore verified by
  transforms, not by an image. Dirt and circuit scenarios were not attempted.
- Software-GL stills understate and overstate specular detail relative to a GPU; fixes were material-level
  (roughness/diffuse liner) so they do not depend on the renderer.
- The 14 unchanged baselines and the 7 bikes' baselines were deleted from the repo after diffing; re-run
  the harness with `--prefix before` on a checkout of a074ea5 to regenerate them.
