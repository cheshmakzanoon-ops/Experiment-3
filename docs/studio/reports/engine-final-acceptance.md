### Paired steady lean and directional force review

The native GP roll equation includes the yaw moment of both spinning wheels. The prior supported path conversion uses only lateral force divided by normal reaction, so it omits that existing physical moment. The coordinator's mirrored, load-scaled equilibrium receipts show about 3.07% less steady lateral acceleration than the requested path at zero hangoff. A separate nominal-limit receipt identifies a second conversion error: dividing the friction budget by a gyroscopic factor before taking its arctangent yields about 4.38% less force than the same authored budget under its explicitly nominal, zero-hangoff reference. These are equation-consistency counterexamples, not race outcomes.

Assignment 29 independently derives the paired mapping directly from the native roll balance. For combined lean `psi`, rider angle `theta`, normal reaction per mass `R`, and spin ratio `c = H/(v*m*h)`, steady lateral acceleration is `R*sin(psi)/(cos(psi)+c*cos(psi-theta))`. Its inverse is `atan2(a*(1+c*cos(theta)), R-a*c*sin(theta))`. The candidate uses this pair consistently for supported path intent, steady reserves and the positive/negative contact limits. Those limits are correctly asymmetric when the rider hangs to one side. The nominal stick scale uses a rolling-spin reference and remains independent of instantaneous suspension load. Nominal aerodynamic force and normal reaction grow together, preserving the same force margin.

The physical tyre and wheel plant remains byte-identical after removing the two new pure helper functions. No grip, controller gain, actuator limit, race threshold or existing tolerance is increased. The candidate passes 121 native cases in ten files, including 40 new balance tests.
