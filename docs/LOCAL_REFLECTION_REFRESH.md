# Motion-aware local reflections

`ReflectionSystem` refreshes the High/Ultra local cubemap from the vehicle's world-space capture origin. Circuit and Festival renderers retain two 256 × 256 cube targets and submit one ordinary refresh face per advancing presentation frame. A material receives the back target only after all six faces have completed. Every face in that cycle uses its first face's origin, even while the vehicle moves.

## Refresh policy

| Trigger                                                                                                                         | Scheduling                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| At least 12 m from the last completed capture origin                                                                            | Start early after at least 0.5 seconds since the previous start.                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| Changed streamed terrain silhouette or newly loaded Festival scenery                                                            | Queue a refresh with the same minimum interval. Changes during a pending cycle coalesce into a later cycle.                                                                                                                                                        |
| Stationary, unchanged scenery                                                                                                   | Retain the caller's ordinary cadence: circuit 1.5 seconds dry / 0.55 seconds wet; Festival 2 seconds dry / 0.8 seconds wet.                                                                                                                                        |
| Initial capture, explicit travel/reset, reverse seek, large presentation-time jump, subject change, or environment epoch change | Capture all six faces immediately and abandon any unpublished partial cycle.                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| Held presentation time                                                                                                          | Do no ordinary work or repeated failed-capture work. A new explicit reset, subject, or environment can still require an immediate capture.                                                                                                                         |
| Failed capture                                                                                                                  | Retain the previous completed texture and restore renderer/material state. Retry from face zero after the minimum start interval, including failed initial or forced captures. A new explicit invalidation, subject, or environment can permit an immediate retry. |

The threshold uses displacement from the origin of the last completed cube, not path length, speed estimates, wall-clock time, or heading. A world-oriented cubemap does not become stale merely because the vehicle rotates. Using `getWorldPosition` also handles vehicles attached to moving parents.

The 12 m threshold is a refresh trigger, not a maximum spatial lag. At 100 m/s the 0.5-second budget permits 50 m between capture origins, and a one-face cycle needs five further advancing frames before publication. This tradeoff keeps rapid travel from turning the probe into continuous six-face rendering.

For current production intervals, ordinary starts are separated by at least 0.5 seconds. This permits at most two starts per second over time and at most one ordinary face per advancing frame. A completed ordinary cycle submits six faces; mipmaps/PMREM are marked for refresh only after its last face. Initial/discontinuity/environment captures retain their exceptional six-face cost in one frame. These limits describe renderer submissions, not measured GPU time or frame rate. The general API still honours an explicitly requested cadence between 0.25 and 0.5 seconds, using that faster interval as its minimum.

Actor/effect exclusions and sub-texel scenery culling remain unchanged. The first complete capture still includes all static scenery for initial residency. During each capture, reflective materials use their original environment maps instead of reading the local target being rendered. Material maps, visibility, sky gain, shadow updates, XR state, render target, viewport, scissor and mipmap settings are restored after a failed face.

## Diagnostics

Both renderers expose `reflectionProbe` in their diagnostics. `starts` separates attempted immediate, motion, scenery and cadence cycles; `completed` counts published cubes. `pendingFace` and `pendingReason` describe an unfinished back target. `origin`, `displacement`, `capturedAt`, `lastStart` and `lastCompleted` distinguish where a map was captured from when it was published. `sceneryPending` shows a revision awaiting a complete capture. `immediateFaces` documents the exceptional six-face submission, and `immediateRetryPending` exposes a failed immediate request awaiting retry.

## Verification

`tests/reflections.test.ts` drives the production `ReflectionSystem` and Three.js cube cameras through controlled renderer submissions. It checks sustained travel at 100 m/s for three seconds: six ordinary starts spaced 0.5 seconds apart, five complete sliced refreshes, one pending cycle at the endpoint, and no more than one ordinary face per advancing frame. It also checks unchanged stationary dry/wet cadence, held frames, world-space parent movement, scenery changes during every pending face, teleport/reverse seek, bounded initial and sliced failure recovery, material ownership, renderer state restoration, and no publication of partial cubes.

These tests establish scheduling and state contracts. Hardware captures remain necessary to assess visual quality and GPU cost.
