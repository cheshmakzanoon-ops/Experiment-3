## Validation-cost boundary

A cloud Node24 source-array benchmark over the unchanged six original payloads
(86,322 numbers) measured repeated copying/JSON comparison versus direct numeric
snapshot comparison. Both rejected the same in-place mutation. That motivated
removing serialization and redundant temporary copies, not reducing check
frequency or caching a successful readiness result. It is not a full-receipt,
native, GPU or startup-time guarantee. All identity/value/mutation guards and
fresh-frame requirements remain mandatory.
